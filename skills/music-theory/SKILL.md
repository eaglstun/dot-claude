# Music theory

Reason from the music in front of you rather than applying complexity by default.
Identify the tonal center, mode, chord functions, melody constraints, bass motion,
and arrangement density before recommending changes.

Preserve the user's style target. Plain triads and power chords are often the
right answer; extensions are useful when they improve color, tension, release,
or voice leading. Explain proposed changes in both functional and audible terms.

Read [references/harmony-and-chord-color.md](references/harmony-and-chord-color.md)
when analyzing or proposing chord extensions, substitutions, borrowed harmony,
slash chords, passing diminished chords, or jazz color in rock/pop writing.

For guitar voicings, tablature, instrument tuning, or OWNER/OPERATORS chart
files, use the separate `guitar` skill.
